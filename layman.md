CyberBench is a command-line tool that lets you encode, decode, and transform text or data using common techniques like Base64, hexadecimal, URL encoding, ROT13, gzip compression, and XOR — all without needing a web browser or an internet connection. You chain operations together in sequence, so you can, for example, Base64-encode some text and then convert it to hex in one command. It also has a "magic" mode that automatically tries to figure out what encoding your data is in and decodes it for you. It is useful for security researchers, developers, and students who frequently need to inspect or manipulate encoded data during day-to-day work.
